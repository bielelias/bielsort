# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest.

Participants must not engage in harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
or deliberate disruption. Critique code and claims rather than people.

Performance work must be reproducible, and disagreements should be resolved
with tests, measurements, and documented reasoning.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when
necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to Gabriel Fernandes Farah Elias at
`gabriel_elias@msn.com`. Include relevant links or context, but do not send
credentials or unrelated personal information. Reports will be reviewed as
privately as practical, and proportionate corrective action may be taken.
